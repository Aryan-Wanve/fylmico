# Fylmico Product Principles

## Product Promise

Fylmico is the operating system for creative production. It should help
creative professionals move from first idea to final delivery inside one
coherent workspace.

## Core Philosophy

- Fylmico is not a generic project management tool.
- Fylmico is not a Notion clone.
- Fylmico is not a Discord clone.
- Fylmico is not a Trello clone.
- Fylmico is not a Frame.io clone.
- Every feature must feel native to creative production.
- Nothing should feel bolted on.

## Product Rules

- Every feature must reduce friction.
- Everything must connect naturally.
- Avoid feature bloat.
- Build desktop-first while remaining fully responsive.
- Design dark-mode-first.
- Prefer professional over flashy.
- AI should assist creativity, not replace creative judgment.
- Users are creative professionals with real deadlines and clients.
- The platform should support freelancers and large production teams.

## Design Principles

- Premium
- Minimal
- Professional
- Elegant
- Fast
- Responsive
- Modern
- Calm
- Intentional

Avoid clutter, childish gradients, template UI, noisy animations, and decorative
elements that do not support the workflow.

## Experience Principles

- Users should always know where a project stands.
- Communication should stay connected to the work it discusses.
- Assets, feedback, approvals, and versions should be traceable.
- Client-facing workflows should feel polished and trustworthy.
- Internal team workflows should feel fast and operationally clear.
