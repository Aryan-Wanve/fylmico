# Glossary

## Workspace

The product environment where an organization manages its creative production
work.

## Organization

The top-level tenant boundary. Organizations own members, teams, clients,
projects, assets, permissions, billing data, and audit logs.

## Member

A user who belongs to an organization.

## Team

A group of organization members who collaborate together, such as editors,
producers, designers, or social media managers.

## Department

A larger organizational unit that can group teams or responsibilities.

## Client

An external customer or stakeholder connected to one or more projects.

## Project

A production workspace for a film, campaign, shoot, content batch, or creative
deliverable.

## Asset

A file or media item used in production, review, delivery, or reference.

## Review

A workflow for collecting comments, approvals, and version feedback on assets.

## Approval

A formal decision that a task, asset, deliverable, or version is accepted.

## Version

A tracked iteration of an asset, document, or deliverable.

## Call Sheet

A production document that communicates shoot schedule, location, crew, cast,
equipment, and operational details.

## Shot List

A planned list of shots for a production.

## Storyboard

A visual sequence that communicates planned scenes or shots.

## Moodboard

A curated visual reference board for tone, style, or creative direction.

## Role

A named set of permissions assigned to a member within an organization or
project context.

## Permission

A specific ability to perform an action on a resource.

## Audit Log

An immutable record of important actions performed in the platform.
